#How Payments Work

## For Clients 

When you find a service you want, you send a booking request and wait for the provider to accept it. Once they confirm, you'll see a "Pay now" button on the booking that's when you pay. The money comes out of your wallet balance, so you'll need to add funds first through the Wallet page (it's basically a prepaid balance you top up before booking anything). When you pay, that money doesn't go straight to the provider  it gets held while the work happens, so you're covered if something goes wrong before the job is done.

Once the provider marks the job as delivered, you'll see a prompt to release the payment. Hit "Release payment" and the funds go to them. If something comes up before delivery provider noshows, job gets cancelled, whatever you can cancel the booking and get a full refund back to your wallet. After delivery though, the release is on you, so do it once you're happy with the work.

---

## For Providers/Getting Paid

When a client pays for your service, that money goes into escrow not your wallet. You won't see it in your available balance yet. Your job is to actually do the work: first mark the booking as "In progress" when you start, then "Delivered" when you're done. That's what triggers the client to release payment on their end.

Once the client confirms, the funds hit your wallet balance and you can withdraw from there — minimum $10, takes 3–5 business days to process. Keep in mind the platform takes a 10% fee at the time of payment, so what lands in your wallet is 90% of the listed price. The escrow section in your wallet shows what's currently held and waiting on client approval, so you always know what's coming.
